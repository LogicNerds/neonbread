GRANDMA THEFT AUTOMATIC

Thanksgiving is Thursday and your table has 100 empty chairs. You have four days to borrow every grandma in town before Big Bertha's Bingo Hall gets them all.

TIP: TURN YOUR PHONE SIDEWAYS FOR A WIDER VIEW

HOW TO PLAY

TOUCH
◀ ▶ steer, GAS goes forward, REVERSE slows you down and backs up, CAKE throws fruitcake, HONK honks, II pauses (sound, music and gore are in the pause menu).
KEYS
Arrow keys or WASD drive. Space or F throws fruitcake, H honks, P pauses, M mutes everything, N turns music on or off.
PAD
Left stick or D-pad steers. RT or A for gas (squeeze lightly to creep), LT or B to brake. X or RB throws fruitcake, Y honks. Start pauses.
GRAB
Roll up slowly. Too fast and you knock her over. Purse grannies need a full stop.
HOME
The only way to fill seats. Each grandma brings guests: most fill 1 chair, purse, mall-walker and knitting grandmas fill 2, high rollers 3, golden grandmas 5.
DEN
Grandmas play bingo and earn Bingo Bucks for van upgrades. No seats.
HEAT
Grabbing grandmas sends glowing grandsons after you. They steal grandmas back. Any drop-off clears your heat.
CAKE
Fruitcake locks on to the nearest grandson. He stops to eat it and your heat drops a bar. Grandmas bake more at home.
TRAFFIC
Locals are out running errands. Hitting a car upsets your passengers.
