PRIVACY

Neon Bread Arcade · Last updated November 27, 2026

You never need an account to play anything here. This page explains what we keep if you choose to sign in, and what we keep if you don't.

IF YOU DON'T SIGN IN

  • Your tickets, prizes, game saves and settings stay in your browser's storage on your device. We never see them.
  • Clearing your browser data erases them.
  • If you put a score on a leaderboard, we store the name you typed (up to 6 letters or numbers), the score, the game and the date. Leaderboards are public.

IF YOU SIGN IN

Signing in is optional. It lets your stuff follow you to other devices. When you sign in we store:

  • Your sign-in. Your email address and an account ID, handled by Google Firebase Authentication. If you use Google, Google tells us your email address and name. We never get your password.
  • Your arcade stuff. Your ticket balance, prizes, prize settings, daily challenge progress, leaderboard name, best scores and game saves.

That's it. We don't run ads, we don't sell or share your information, and we don't use it for anything except saving your stuff.

WHERE IT LIVES

Account data is stored in Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore). Google processes it for us under its own security and privacy terms. Only you can read or change your saved data.

AGE

You must be 13 or older to sign in. Anyone can play without an account. If you believe a child under 13 has signed in, delete the account from the arcade (see below) and it's gone.

DELETING YOUR ACCOUNT

In the arcade, tap SAVED ✓ at the top of the screen or at the prize counter, then DELETE MY ACCOUNT. That erases your saved tickets, prizes and game progress from our servers right away, removes your sign-in, and clears your device. Scores already posted to the public leaderboards stay up, because they aren't tied to your account.

SIGNING OUT

Signing out keeps everything in your account and clears it from that device until you sign in again.

CHANGES

If this page changes, the date at the top changes with it.
